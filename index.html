  1. Day 1

    Agree on the terms.

    Complete the open employment details and sign the final agreement. The window begins.

  2. Through day 90

    Choose the starting point.

    Employment first sets 15/5. A qualifying client first sets 20/5, with employment due within 10 business days.

  3. At the end of the window

    Keep the outcome clear.

    If neither path activates, the hiring commitment expires. Defined grace and commission protections preserve work already in motion.

What about a deal still in progress? +

A full-build contract approved and signed by both parties by day 90 has a proposed 30-day grace period for its first payment to clear. That can activate the client-first path.

A separate 90-day protection applies to registered prospects acknowledged by Handrail as active at expiry. A full-build sale that qualifies within that protection period can earn commission without reviving the hiring obligation outside the payment grace. These are proposed protections detailed in the agreement.
