## Mobile tour design review

For changes to mobile camera framing, printed layout or scroll choreography, require an independent adversarial design review of current rendered scenes before publication. The reviewer must challenge complete-idea visibility, related headings/copy, optical zoom, spacing, peripheral context and browser-chrome occlusion. A passing selected-element bounding-box test is insufficient. Record findings and their disposition in the current remediation document; reject or revise the candidate when primary copy is clipped or an isolated sentence becomes a giant crop.

Keep deterministic coverage for complete reading-group text lines inside the measured header/control safe area, small and short phone viewports, and height changes that preserve place while refitting content. Preserve the existing high-density paper budget. Device emulation is not physical iPhone certification.
