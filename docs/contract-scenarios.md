# Proposal verification

This is a negotiable business proposal, not a final contract. The earlier draft's tie rule, tails, grace periods and administrative deadlines were removed at the user's direction. They are not implemented or agreed terms.

The canonical notes live in src/content/proposal.ts. HTML, generated Markdown and the PDF use those exports. Four unit tests cover document structure, stable section links, complete ordered content and independent arithmetic for the payment illustration. The browser suite checks the actual rendered copy and download; Poppler independently verifies every PDF paragraph in order.

| Proposed path | Build commission | Recurring commission | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Handrail hires first | 15% of collections | 5% of collections | My credited sales |
| I bring the qualifying client first | 20% of collections | 5% of collections | Triggering client and all future credited sales |

A $120,000 build collected over 12 equal installments produces $10,000 monthly collections. At 20%, commission is $2,000 and $8,000 remains before delivery, benefits and other costs. At 15%, those amounts are $1,500 and $8,500. A $2,000 recurring collection produces $100 commission at 5%. These are illustrations, not Handrail pricing or sales forecasts.

The proposal describes a 90-day opportunity and a benefits request. Qualifying-client criteria, attribution, recurring duration, benefits details and handling of deals already in progress remain discussion items for the final agreement. No public interaction signs or accepts the proposal.
