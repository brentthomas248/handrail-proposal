# Camera glide between reading scenes

The user requested that transitions feel like the camera moving across a stationary flyer, with a slight pullback and return. This is a bounded motion correction; the opening and final closing fold, landing compositions, timing and commercial copy stay in place.

The former crease transfers changed the paper hinges from 38° to 62°, rocked the roll and overshot the reading bearing. The camera-relative diffuse light also relit the page as the view turned. The reading path now holds both folds at 38°, keeps roll at zero, limits bearings to the actual reading faces and removes the long vertical detour through the middle of the paper. Same-panel travel gains an 8% midpoint pullback; crossed creases retain a 10% pullback. Both return to the existing readable landing scale. Matte diffuse light is fixed in paper coordinates; facing/culling still uses the actual camera normal.

Validation before implementation: three updated unit assertions fail the old hinge path; both new browser regressions fail the published build (same-panel scale never pulls back; the crossed wing moves above38°). The corrected source passes all 34 unit tests, typecheck across 52 files and the three-route build. Independent rendered review accepts all 17 reading arrivals at desktop, 390px and 320px; 2,582 captured reading frames retain fixed folds. All 14 focused Chromium and two supplementary WebKit checks pass without retries. The checks include prompt timing, forward/reverse glides, stable matte light, cold opening paint/layout, closing ink/terminal behavior and the inclusive material budget. Publication and hosted verification are next. Earlier wide design scores are historical; physical iPhone playback is unverified.
