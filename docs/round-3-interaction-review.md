# Round-three interaction and inclusive-design review

Independent review of the local rendered candidate on 27 September 2026, including proposal, notes and the new resume. This review owns navigation, input, reading alternatives and focus visibility; spatial art direction and resume editorial quality have separate specialists. The implementation author did not supply the screenshots used for this review.

## Research and method

The [round-three research](design-research-round-3.md) establishes the primary sources. This reviewer directly inspected W3C's [Focus Not Obscured minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html), [Reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html) and [Target Size Minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html) guidance. The review distinguishes AA minimums from stronger project quality requirements: 24px targets have exceptions, and a partly clipped focus ring is not equivalent to a completely hidden focused component. Apple motion guidance informs interruption and reverse-input checks; this is not blanket WCAG or Apple conformance certification.

Fresh Chromium pages were exercised using native Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, wheel, click, browser Back and full navigation. Initial route coverage used 320×740, 390×664 and 1440×1000; tablet header checks added 760, 768, 820, 960, 1024 and 1199px. Evidence is ignored under `qa-artifacts/round-3/interaction/`. These are browser checks, not physical iPhone or manual screen-reader evidence.

## Other observed behavior

- Native keyboard traversal across the nine route/viewport combinations reached visible header and document actions. Chapter traversal stayed in tour mode; paper-link focus exposed ordinary reading. The special multiline notes-link bounding boxes include whitespace between lines; that whitespace was not misreported as an occluding element.
- Native wheel input, immediate reversal, a 1.8-second pause and renewed input worked at 320, 390 and 1440px. The camera transform changed during input; settling continued in the last chosen direction rather than undoing the reversal. Read normally / Take the tour remained functional afterward. Measurements: `motion-input.json` (scroll coordinates and actual transforms; the root CSS progress field in these scripts is not used as evidence).
- No horizontal overflow or uncaught page errors occurred in those nine surfaces. Axe with WCAG 2 A/AA, 2.1 AA and 2.2 AA tags reported no violations. That automated result supplements the input and rendered inspection.
- At 320px, no-JavaScript, reduced-motion and explicit ordinary-reading paths retained the complete proposal/resume/notes headings and content. The skip link bypassed header navigation and subsequent Tab reached the first actual content action.
- Both resume and proposal downloads returned HTTP 200 with PDF signatures through all three reading alternatives. The resume's decorative watermarks do not enter the accessible name of the document. PDF typography and page composition are owned by the editorial specialist.
- Normal-reading resume excursions retained the exact browser scroll position on Back: 2656px at 390px, 1960px at 1440px. The history finding concerns the animated tour specifically.

## Acceptance boundary

All four actionable findings are independently closed. This bounded interaction review accepts the corrected candidate for release, subject to the remaining specialists and the normal release checks. Physical Safari process stability, manual VoiceOver, hardware touch and general WCAG conformance are not established by this review. No app code, tests, build output or public profile was changed by this reviewer.
